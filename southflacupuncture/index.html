How we write

Honest about what we know.

Health sites love big promises. We would rather tell you that the evidence for some things is solid, for others mixed, and for a few not there yet.

  • Every health claim links to a health agency or a published study
  • No cure language, ever, and no miracle stories
  • Safety and medication questions come first
  • When something is outside acupuncture, we say so

Meet the author

Dr. Jonathan M. Fields, DAOM, L.Ac.

Dr. Fields trained in the United States and China, including time at the Shaolin Temple, and has taught qigong and tai chi for more than twenty years.
