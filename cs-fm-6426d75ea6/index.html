Meet Dr. Fields: my story

Before I practiced functional medicine, I was a patient. I struggled with serious health problems of my own, and conventional medicine had very little to offer me. At one point I was disabled by a dozen debilitating symptoms. I know how it feels to be that stuck.

I've been studying Eastern integrated medicine for over 22 years. And I went back to school full-time to be able to practice functional medicine. Through diet, lifestyle, functional medicine, acupuncture, and herbs, I made a full recovery within six to eight months. That experience is a big part of why I practice the way I do.

This is my story, and I share it so you know where I am coming from. Your body and your situation are your own, so I can’t promise you the same result. What I can tell you is that I take feeling stuck seriously, with every person who walks in. Dr. Jonathan M. Fields, DAOM

A different kind of first conversation

Your first visit with Dr. Jonathan M. Fields, DAOM, is mostly listening. He wants the whole timeline: when things changed, what was going on in your life at the time, what you eat on a real weekday, and how you sleep. He also asks what has already been tried. Many Coral Springs patients juggle school drop offs, a commute on the Sawgrass, and long workdays, so the plan has to fit that life. A plan you cannot follow is not a plan.

Bring what you already have

There is no need to start from zero. Bring:

  • Recent lab reports from your primary care doctor, OB/GYN or endocrinologist.
  • A list of your medications and any supplements in your cabinet.
  • A few days of notes on food, energy and sleep, if you can.

Dr. Fields reads these as a set and looks for connections that are easy to miss when each specialist sees only one piece. When more testing makes sense, he explains why, so you can decide with your own physician.

What is functional medicine?

Think of it as detective work. Instead of only managing a symptom, we try to find out what is driving it. We start with your bloodwork and add whatever else helps fill in the picture: a gut microbiome screening, nutrient levels, toxin exposure, genetics, food sensitivities and specialty labs. Then we build a plan around you, with medical grade supplements, food choices and daily habits that fit your body. The aim is to get at the cause of a long-running problem while we also support the symptoms.

What a typical first visit looks like

First visits can feel unfamiliar, so here is the walk-through. Nothing here should surprise you.

Before you come in

You can fill out the new patient form, medical history and consent online. If you would rather do it at the office, just arrive 15 minutes early. You will also get a couple of questionnaires to complete.

The conversation

We will ask about your condition, your sleep, your energy, your digestion, how you sweat, what you eat and how you live. Yes, we ask about bowel movements and urination too. It can feel a little odd at first, but those answers help a lot. We will also look at your tongue, take your pulse and sometimes check your blood pressure.

Your plan

Once we have the full picture, we explain what we think is going on and lay out a plan, including the benefits and what to expect, before anything starts. Depending on your needs and my judgment, that plan might include diet and lifestyle changes, medical grade supplements, herbs, acupuncture and maybe other therapies.

After that

Most people see us about once a month. We go over how you are doing, look at any new test results and adjust the plan as your situation changes.

What to bring

A list of your medications and supplements, any labs and medical reports from the last couple of years, and your questions.

How long will it take to feel better?

It varies, and we would rather be honest about that than hand you a promise. A lot depends on how your body responds and how long the problem has been around.

Something recent can respond within a few weeks. A long-standing condition usually takes longer, and it may be 3 to 6 months before the changes feel meaningful. In our general experience, a typical patient needs about 3 to 4 months to start feeling better and more in control. That is a rough pattern we have seen, not a guarantee, and your own timeline could be shorter or longer.

Sometimes the realistic goal is better quality of life and fewer symptoms. After your first consultation we will map out a schedule so you know what to expect. Some patients tell us they respond well, and others see smaller changes.

Frequently asked questions

What is functional medicine, really?

It is a way of using your lab work, gut microbiome screening, toxins, nutrient levels, food sensitivities and genetics to look for imbalances that may be behind your symptoms and that standard testing may not flag.

We pay attention to patterns and borderline lab results that are easy to miss in a routine visit. Nutrient shortfalls, toxin exposure, diet, exercise, sleep, mold, Lyme and other infections, and stress all get a look. From there we recommend specific medical grade supplements, herbs and vitamins to work on the underlying cause and the symptoms you feel day to day.

People often ask what we work with. Autoimmune conditions, thyroid, hormones, adrenal fatigue, infertility, OB/GYN issues, digestion, chronic pain, brain fog, skin, allergies, stress and anxiety, sleep disorders, and plenty more.

Which conditions do you work with?

A wide range. People come to us for natural, supportive and adjunctive care with chronic fatigue, thyroid, digestive disorders, hormonal imbalances, autoimmune conditions, weight loss, menopause, fertility, anxiety, depression, stress, headache, fibromyalgia, insomnia and other sleep issues, painful and irregular periods, addiction, PMS, mental and emotional health, gastrointestinal and immune concerns, allergy, cardiovascular health, dementia, arthritis, diabetes, slow development in children, ADD/ADHD, vertigo, gout, high cholesterol, acne, asthma, bloating, vomiting, ulcers, reflux, IBS, IBD, Crohn’s disease, ulcerative colitis, eczema, cysts, PCOS, low libido, urinary issues, tonsillitis, UTIs, constipation and diarrhea, and much more.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Bring your medications, supplements, recent labs and questions, and arrive early if you have not done the paperwork online. We cover the whole visit step by step in the section above.

Is it safe?

Your health and safety come first, every time. If our protocols or the office environment worry you, tell us. We want to hear it.

Our clinicians are well trained and have at least ten years of experience. We use only medical grade supplements and herbs that have been tested by a third party. Our therapists hold Florida Department of Health licenses, and they carry national board certification through the NCCAOM.

Herbs, vitamins and supplements are generally considered quite safe, and they tend to have few side effects compared with over-the-counter and prescription drugs. Most of our herbal formulas are sold only to licensed practitioners and are lab tested for quality, heavy metals and pesticides. If you ever feel uncomfortable or have a concern, stop taking your formula and contact your practitioner right away.

Before any treatment, our staff will walk you through its risks and benefits.

How long does it take to work?

It comes down to how your body responds and how serious the condition is. We cover this in the section above, and the short version is that it varies. Right after your first consultation we will give you a schedule to work from.

More questions we hear a lot

Do I have to stop my medications?

No. I won’t ask you to stop a prescription. Bring your medication list so we can plan around it, and keep working with your own physician.

Will this replace my regular doctor?

No. Functional medicine and acupuncture here support your medical care and do not replace it. I am glad to share notes you can take to your physicians, so everyone works from the same picture.

I have lab results from my doctor. Should I bring them?

Please do. Bring reports from the last couple of years if you have them, from your primary care doctor, OB/GYN, endocrinologist or anyone else. When more testing makes sense, I explain why, so you can decide with your own physician.

Who will I see?

You see me at every visit. I take your history, write your plan and follow how it is going, so you never have to repeat your story to someone new.

How often will we meet?

Usually about once a month. We use that time to go over your progress, review new test results and update the plan.

I have already tried everything. Is there any point?

We hear that story a lot. If you have seen dozens of doctors and still feel stuck, bring your records and your story. We will tell you honestly whether functional medicine is a good fit.

Will acupuncture be part of my plan?

It might be. Depending on your needs, a plan can include acupuncture along with diet and lifestyle changes, supplements, herbs and sometimes other therapies. We choose what fits you.
