Dr. Nadav Fields

Medical Director

Dr. Nadav Fields is the co-founder and medical director of CR8 Health. Dr. Fields received his bachelor’s degree from the University of Florida and completed his master’s degree in Biomedicine at Florida Atlantic University. He graduated from Touro College of Medicine New York and completed his residency training in Internal Medicine at the University of Arizona.

He is double board-certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine and the American Board of Obesity Medicine. Dr. Fields is trained in Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy by the Preventative & Innovative Medicine Academy. Additionally, he is certified in Peptide Therapy and completed training with Dr. William Seeds, the world’s leader in Cellular and Peptide Therapy.

“Throughout his career, Dr. Nadav has worked in the emergency department, general medical ward, and in intensive care unit settings. It was here that he witnessed the sequelae of poor nutrition and lifestyle choices, which resulted in accelerated aging, obesity, heart disease, strokes, and premature death.

With growing frustrations in our healthcare system where we mostly focus on treating current disease states and alleviating symptoms, Dr. Fields became increasingly interested in Preventative Health with a focus on proactive not reactive medicine. This path eventually led him to opening CR8 Health where he can now engage, educate and empower his patient’s to reach their health destiny.”

Nurse Practitioner

Jena Brusilovsky APRN-C is a board-certified nurse practitioner specializing in Adult Primary Care and Aesthetic Medicine. Dr. Daylin Shelley

DAHM

Dr. Daylin Shelley, DAHM, is a South Florida native who believes that food is medicine and that dis-ease is rooted in spirit. She is a Doctor of Acupuncture & Herbal Medicine and a functional medicine practitioner. She is licensed both in Florida and Washington state, and is nationally certified by the NCBAHM (National Certification Board for Acupuncture & Herbal Medicine). She also has advanced studies in a distal needling technique (where the needles are placed in areas away from the location of discomfort) known as the Balance Method, as well as Classical Chinese Medicine and Extraordinary Vessels. She has a deep interest in treating autoimmune disorders, gut issues, women’s health, and chronic pain.

Dr. Daylin has spent precious time studying under Dr. Ashley Herrin, the founder and original owner of Vibrant Health Center, and is dedicated to continuing the important and beautiful work that Dr. Herrin has cultivated. Dr. Daylin’s own journey with Crohn’s disease inspired her commitment to helping others restore balance through acupuncture, food, and lifestyle changes.

She is a graduate of Bastyr University, with a Bachelor of Science in Nutrition and Culinary Arts, a Master of Oriental Medicine from the Atlantic Institute of Oriental Medicine, and a Doctorate in Acupuncture and Herbal Medicine from Daoist Traditions College of Chinese Medical Arts. She is also a certified Sivananda yoga and yoga nidra instructor, bringing a deep mind-body awareness into her work. Dr. Daylin is dedicated to empowering her patients with individualized, inclusive, heart-centered care and believes acupuncture has the power to transform lives.

Monica Valencia

Aesthetician

Monica Valencia was born in Colombia and is bilingual in Spanish and English. She lives in Boynton Beach and has two beautiful daughters. She started her career working in the med spa industry 23 years ago. Monica’s exposure to this industry sparked her passion for skincare treatments. In 2006, Monica attended the Florida College of Natural Health for her Aesthetician license. A few years later, she attended the Boca Beauty Academy for her education and certification on laser technology and electrolysis. Her expertise, training, and certifications allow her to perform laser and facial treatments including chemical peels, medical-grade facials, dermaplaning, morpheus, photofacials, and more. “The love I have for all things beauty-related allows me to enjoy every second of what I do” says Monica. “I never feel like I am at work. I believe that the aesthetics industry constantly evolves and offers an ongoing learning experience that I truly enjoy”
