At CR8, our facial treatments are designed to deliver more than relaxation — they are structured, results-driven skin therapies. We combine advanced modalities, corrective ingredients, and intentional treatment layering to improve tone, texture, clarity, and firmness at a deeper level.


Every service is customized to your skin’s condition and long-term goals, whether you’re maintaining healthy glow, correcting pigmentation, stimulating collagen, or addressing acne and inflammation.


This is luxury skincare backed by clinical precision — where every treatment is purposeful and every result is measurable.

Our facial treatments deliver a refined balance of clinical precision and restorative care. The result is skin that appears visibly renewed—smooth, luminous, and revitalized—while advanced techniques promote relaxation and skin vitality.

Led by skilled professionals, each treatment is carefully customized to address individual skin concerns, including acne, fine lines and wrinkles, uneven tone, dryness, sensitivity, congestion, and diminished radiance.

Facial Treatments Menu

At CR8 Health, we offer a variety of advanced facial treatments and skincare solutions, each designed to enhance your skin’s health and beauty, including:

Signature Facials

CR8 Express Reset

A revitalizing facial that cleanses, exfoliates, and hydrates, perfect for dull, dehydrated skin. Leaves your complexion with a refreshed, balanced glow.

CR8 Deep Purifying

A deep-cleansing facial featuring extended extractions and detox masks, ideal for congested, oily skin. It targets blackheads and promotes clearer pores with a smoother, more refined texture.

CR8 HydroJelly Infusion

A rejuvenating facial with NAD+ Longevity and Glutathione, perfect for dry, aging, or hyperpigmented skin. Leaves the skin calm, deeply hydrated, and glowing with renewed vitality.

CR8 Dermaplane Refinement

A targeted exfoliation treatment with peach-fuzz removal, ideal for dull texture and prepping skin for makeup. Leaves skin brighter, smoother, and with an ultra-refined finish.

Glow Infusion Therapies

CR8 Collagen Induction Therapy

Precision microneedling targets fine lines, enlarged pores, and acne scars, promoting firmer skin and smoother texture. A series of treatments is recommended for optimal results.

 
 

CR8 Radiance Infusion

A deep cleanse followed by infusion hydration, perfect for combating dullness and dehydration. Leaves skin with an instant glow and a plump, refreshed appearance.

CR8 NanoGlow Infusion

Nano-channeling treatment with no downtime, ideal for skin maintenance and hydration. Provides an immediate, radiant glow.

CR8 Plasma Renewal

Advanced regenerative technology targeting aging and uneven texture. Delivers revitalized, smoother, and more refined skin.

Corrective Skin & Peel Therapies

CR8 Clear Skin Acne

A multi-layer acne treatment with LED therapy, designed to target breakouts and inflammation. Results in clearer, calmer skin with continued improvement over time.

CR8 Calm & Restore

A soothing treatment focused on barrier repair and redness reduction, ideal for rosacea and reactive skin. Leaves skin strengthened, calmed, and visibly more even-toned.

CR8 Liquid Lift Collagen

Needle-free collagen bio-stimulation targets skin laxity, uneven texture, and signs of aging. Promotes a firmer, smoother appearance, with a recommended program for best results.

CR8 BioRenew Radiance Peel

The Advanced Bi-Phase Peel (BioRePeel) targets sun damage, acne scars, and pigmentation. It leaves the skin with a brighter tone and renewed, smoother texture.

Advanced Anti-Aging

CR8 Collagen Renewal

A treatment combining exfoliation, restorative masks, and LED therapy, designed to target fine lines and loss of elasticity. Leaves skin firmer, more youthful, and rejuvenated.

CR8 Lift & Sculpt

Microcurrent stimulation with contouring techniques targets sagging and loss of definition. Delivers a lifted, sculpted appearance for a more youthful contour.

CR8 Cryolift

Cold therapy to tighten and calm, ideal for reducing puffiness, redness, and inflammation. Leaves the complexion refreshed, tightened, and more even-toned.
