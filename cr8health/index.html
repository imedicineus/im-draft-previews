Meet The Team

At CR8 Health, our team blends expertise in both wellness and aesthetics to provide a holistic approach to your care. With a focus on enhancing both your inner health and outer beauty, we work together to deliver personalized treatments that help you look and feel your best. Our experienced professionals, from skilled practitioners to welcoming support staff, are dedicated to creating a comfortable and transformative experience. With cutting-edge treatments and a commitment to excellence, CR8 Health is here to help you achieve your aesthetic and wellness goals.

Dr. Nadav Fields

Medical Director
Read info

Dr. Nadav Fields is the co-founder and medical director of CR8 Health. Dr. Fields received his bachelor’s degree from the University of Florida and completed his master’s degree in Biomedicine at Florida Atlantic University. He graduated from Touro College of Medicine New York and completed his residency training in Internal Medicine at the University of Arizona.

He is double board-certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine and the American Board of Obesity Medicine. Dr. Fields is trained in Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy by the Preventative & Innovative Medicine Academy. Additionally, he is certified in Peptide Therapy and completed training with Dr. William Seeds, the world’s leader in Cellular and Peptide Therapy.

“Throughout his career, Dr. Nadav has worked in the emergency department, general medical ward, and in intensive care unit settings. It was here that he witnessed the sequelae of poor nutrition and lifestyle choices, which resulted in accelerated aging, obesity, heart disease, strokes, and premature death.

With growing frustrations in our healthcare system where we mostly focus on treating current disease states and alleviating symptoms, Dr. Fields became increasingly interested in Preventative Health with a focus on proactive not reactive medicine. This path eventually led him to opening CR8 Health where he can now engage, educate and empower his patient’s to reach their health destiny.”

Monica Valencia

Aesthetician

Monica Valencia was born in Colombia and is bilingual in Spanish and English. She lives in Boynton Beach and has two beautiful daughters. She started her career working in the med spa industry 23 years ago. Monica’s exposure to this industry sparked her passion for skincare treatments. In 2006, Monica attended the Florida College of Natural Health for her Aesthetician license. Her expertise, training, and certifications allow her to perform laser and facial treatments including chemical peels, medical-grade facials, dermaplaning, morpheus, photofacials, and more. “The love I have for all things beauty-related allows me to enjoy every second of what I do” says Monica. “I never feel like I am at work.
