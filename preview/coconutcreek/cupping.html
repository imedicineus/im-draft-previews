Cupping in Coconut Creek, FL

Cupping uses suction to lift skin and soft tissue. At the Coconut Creek clinic it is often paired with acupuncture for backs, necks and shoulders that feel stuck after work, sports or long drives.

The Short Version

Cups create a steady pull that many people describe as a deep stretch. Marks can look like round bruises and usually fade over several days. Aftercare is simple: hydration, easy movement and sun sense while color settles.

02

Where It Tends to Help

Desk stiffness, training recovery and areas that feel glued down are common reasons families ask for cupping. It is a supportive method inside a broader plan.

03

What to Expect

Skin is prepared, cups are applied and you rest briefly. Stationary, moving or flash techniques may be used. Tell Dr. Fields if suction feels too strong so it can be eased.

04

About the Marks

Marks are temporary and are not a score of how "toxic" the body is. Darker color often shows over tighter tissue. Keep the area comfortable while it fades.

05

Safety

Cupping is avoided over broken skin, infection and in some pregnancy situations. Share blood thinner use and bruising history before cups go on.

Glass, Pump or Silicone

Research Notes

Studies suggest possible short term relief for some neck and back complaints. The evidence base is still growing, so cupping is offered as a traditional manual option with clear limits.

Pairing Cups With Other Care

Cups often prepare tissue before needles or settle an area afterward. The choice follows the exam rather than a one size menu.

Aftercare at Home

Loose shirts help while color fades. Skip intense heat or heavy loading on treated tissue for a day if it feels tender. Easy walking is welcome.

Comfort Comes First

Less suction is always available on request. Stronger is not automatically better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will cupping fit my situation in Coconut Creek?

After history and exam Dr. Fields will say whether cupping is a reasonable next step or whether another path makes more sense first.

How soon might I notice changes?

Some people feel easier motion within a few visits. Stubborn patterns often need a longer arc. Progress is reassessed on a set schedule rather than guessed.

Is the first visit different?

Yes. Visit one emphasizes listening, exam and a clear plan. Treatment may begin that day when appropriate, always with your okay.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing that lets the clinician reach the area being treated. Athletic layers work well for most Coconut Creek patients.
