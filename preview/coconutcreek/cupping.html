Cupping in Coconut Creek: Five Things to Know

You may have seen round marks on athletes' backs on TV and wondered what they were. That is cupping, a traditional therapy that uses suction to lift skin and muscle. Dr. Jonathan M. Fields offers cupping at our Coconut Creek clinic, often as part of an acupuncture visit.

1. It Feels Like a Deep Stretch

Massage pushes down on muscles. Cupping pulls up. Most people describe it as a deep, comfortable stretch. Suction is adjusted to your comfort.

02

2. The Marks Are Temporary

Cups can leave round pink, red or purple marks. They come from small capillaries near the surface releasing a little blood under suction. They usually do not hurt and fade within about one to two weeks. Plan around pool parties and family photos if marks would bother you.

03

3. There Are Different Styles

Stationary cups stay in place for a few minutes. Moving cups glide across oiled skin like a massage. Flash cupping places and removes cups quickly and leaves lighter marks.

04

4. Research Is Still Developing

According to the NCCIH, some research suggests cupping may help with pain such as neck and low back pain, but much of the research is low quality and more studies are needed. We offer it as a supportive therapy.

05

5. Aftercare Is Simple

Keep the area covered and warm, drink water and skip hot tubs and strong sun for the rest of the day.

The deeper story

For readers who want the details: the research, the history and the reasoning behind how we work.

Who Uses Cupping

Parents with tight upper backs and shoulders. Athletes with sore muscles between games. Desk workers with neck tension. Anyone who carries stress in their shoulders.

History

Cupping has been used in many cultures for centuries, including China, Egypt, Greece and the Middle East. Early cups were made of horn or bamboo. Today we use glass, plastic or silicone cups.

Glass, Pump or Silicone

Glass cups use a brief flame inside the cup to create suction; the flame never touches your skin. Pump cups allow fine control for sensitive patients. Silicone cups are flexible and work well for moving cupping.

Cupping for Teens

Teens may receive gentle cupping with parental consent and a parent present. Suction is kept light.

When to Skip It

Avoid cupping over broken skin, sunburn, rashes, varicose veins or fresh injuries. Tell us about pregnancy, blood thinners or easy bruising.

Pairing With Other Treatments

Cupping is often combined with acupuncture, gua sha and dry needling.

When to Call Your Doctor Instead

Chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden weakness or numbness, high fever, severe abdominal pain or any symptom that is quickly getting worse needs medical care right away. Call 911 in an emergency.

What You May Notice Afterward

Many people feel looser and more relaxed right away. Some feel a little tired and sleep well that night. The area may feel tender to the touch for a day. If marks seem unusually dark or last more than two weeks, mention it at your next visit.
