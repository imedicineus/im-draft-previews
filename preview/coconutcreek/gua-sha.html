The Short Version

Strokes stay even and measured. A temporary surface flush is common and usually clears in days. Pressure follows your feedback; sharp pain means we ease immediately.

02

Where It Tends to Help

Typical reasons include carpool neck strain, upper back tightness after practices and tension-linked headaches. Facial sessions, when chosen, use a much lighter touch.

03

What to Expect

Lotion or oil is applied, then the tool moves along the tissue. You should feel deep pressure without sharp pain. Warmth afterward is common. Water and easy motion help.

04

About the Marks

Redness or light petechiae can be a normal response. Protect pink skin from strong sun. Call the clinic if marks seem excessive.

05

Safety

Avoid gua sha over broken skin, infection, sunburn and certain bleeding risks. Mention blood thinners and skin conditions before the session.

Facial Versus Body Work

Body gua sha covers larger muscles with firmer strokes. Facial work is lighter. Dr. Fields states which approach he is using before he starts.

Research Snapshot

Early studies report short term help for some neck pain presentations. Findings are treated as supportive, not final proof for every case.

Choosing Gua Sha on a Given Day

When guarding is obvious and skin is healthy, gua sha may lead the manual portion of the visit. Irritable tissue gets a gentler plan instead.

Home Care Afterward

Comfortable clothing, water and no harsh scrubbing are enough for most people. Mild warmth is fine after redness calms.

Pressure Feedback

Feedback during strokes keeps pressure productive. Patients are encouraged to speak up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon might I notice changes?

Some people feel easier motion within a few visits. Stubborn patterns often need a longer arc. Progress is reassessed on a set schedule rather than guessed.

Yes. Visit one emphasizes listening, exam and a clear plan. Treatment may begin that day when appropriate, always with your okay.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing that lets the clinician reach the area being treated. Athletic layers work well for most Coconut Creek patients.
