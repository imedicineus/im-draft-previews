Dry Needling in Parkland, FL

Dry needling is a focused way I treat tight, irritable muscle bands that refer pain and limit motion. Parkland patients who lift, run, sit long hours or recover from sports strains often ask about it. Care is delivered at our nearby Coral Springs and Tamarac clinics.

The Short Version

I locate trigger points, place a fine filament needle into the taut band and look for a brief local twitch response. The goal is to ease the band, improve motion and make your home exercises more effective. It is precise muscle work, not a full traditional acupuncture point prescription.

02

Where It Tends to Help

Common reasons Parkland patients book dry needling include neck and shoulder tightness, low back spasm, hip and glute irritation, tennis elbow style forearm pain and calf or plantar foot tension. It works best as part of a plan that also includes movement, not as a stand-alone fix.

03

What to Expect

After history and exam, I explain which muscles I plan to treat. You may feel a quick ache or twitch when the needle meets the band, then a sense of release. Soreness like a light workout can last a day or two. Hydration, gentle walking and the stretches I show you help.

04

Dry Needling and Traditional Acupuncture

I am trained in both. Dry needling targets myofascial trigger points with a Western anatomical map. Traditional acupuncture uses channel theory and whole person pattern diagnosis. Some visits use one approach; some blend ideas when that serves your goals. I will tell you which plan we are using and why.

05

What the Research Says

Reviews of dry needling for myofascial pain report short term improvements in pain and range of motion for many people, especially when combined with exercise. Study quality varies, so I treat the evidence as promising and still evolving rather than final.

06

Safety

Tell me about pregnancy, bleeding risk, infections, implants and needle anxiety. I use sterile single-use needles and avoid unsafe areas. If you feel sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms, say so immediately.

How It Differs Day to Day

A dry needling visit is often shorter and more localized than a full acupuncture session. You might still receive acupuncture on another day for sleep, stress or a broader pattern. We choose based on what your body needs that week.

Aftercare

Easy movement the same day is usually fine. Avoid heavy training for 24 hours if a treated muscle is sore. Heat or a warm shower can feel good. Call the clinic if soreness seems out of proportion.

Who Asks for This in Parkland

Runners along Holmberg and University, weight room athletes and desk workers with stubborn scapular bands are frequent visitors. I map the referring pattern before any needle goes in so we are not chasing the sore spot alone.

Pairing With Home Exercise

The needle session opens a window. The stretches and activation drills you practice afterward keep that window useful. Skipping homework usually means the band returns sooner.

Session Length and Focus

Dry needling visits stay focused on the mapped muscles. You should leave knowing which bands were treated and which homework protects the gain.
