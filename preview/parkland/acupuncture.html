The Short Version

Acupuncture places very fine, sterile needles at chosen points on the body. I combine traditional Chinese medicine assessment with a modern orthopedic exam and your medical history, then select points for the main complaint and the pattern behind it. Needle retention is commonly about 20 to 30 minutes while you rest on the table.

02

Where It Tends to Help

Parkland patients often come in for back and neck pain, headaches, knee or shoulder pain, sleep trouble, digestive upset and stress that shows up in the body. A large individual patient data meta analysis led by Andrew Vickers in 2018 pooled data from about 20,000 patients and found acupuncture worked better than sham and better than no acupuncture for chronic back and neck pain, osteoarthritis, chronic headache and shoulder pain, with benefits that largely persisted a year later.

The National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health summarizes evidence for several pain conditions and notes acupuncture is generally safe when done by a qualified practitioner.

03

What to Expect

Visit one centers on a careful conversation. I want to know about the problem that brought you in, and also about sleep, digestion, energy, stress and history. Then I examine you, explain what I find and describe the plan before we begin.

The filaments used are roughly hair-thin. Most people feel a brief pinch or nothing at all, followed by a dull ache, warmth or heaviness. Many patients fall asleep on the table. I may add heat, gentle electrical stimulation, cupping or gua sha.

04

How Care Is Planned

Many plans begin with weekly visits across six to eight weeks before we step back and reassess. As things improve, visits space out. I also give simple home steps such as stretches, food ideas or a short qigong routine so progress continues between visits.

05

How It Works With Your Doctors

Acupuncture does not replace your physician. I will always tell you if I think you need medical evaluation first. With your permission I can share notes with your other clinicians.

06

Safety

Sterile, single-use needles are standard. Please tell me about pregnancy, bleeding disorders, pacemakers, implants and all medications and supplements. If something feels sharp or wrong during a session, say so right away so I can adjust.

What the Research Adds

Dr. Helene Langevin's work showed that turning an acupuncture needle engages the connective tissue around it (Langevin et al.). Other researchers described fluid filled spaces within connective tissue that may be relevant (Benias et al.). The classic gate control theory of pain offers a framework for how sensory input can change how pain is felt. None of this is the final word, but it helps explain why a careful needling plan can matter.

What I Ask of You

Bring a list of medications and supplements, prior imaging or lab reports if you have them, and honest notes about what makes the problem better or worse. Wear comfortable clothes. Arrive a few minutes early so we are not rushed.

About Results and Limits

Individual results vary. Acupuncture is not an answer for everything. If your case is outside what I can help with, I will say so and point you toward the right next step.

Local Rhythm in Parkland

Many households here balance school calendars, youth sports and long I-75 or Turnpike drives. That rhythm shows up as tight necks, weekends of catch-up chores and sleep that slips later than planned. Treatment plans account for those patterns instead of pretending every week looks the same.

A Plain Spoken First Plan

At the end of visit one you leave knowing the working idea, the near term visit rhythm and what to do at home. If acupuncture is not the best next step, you will hear that clearly and get a referral direction when I can offer one.

Questions Worth Bringing

Write down when the problem started, what imaging or therapy you already tried and which daily tasks hurt most. Those details sharpen the exam faster than a vague "everything hurts" description.

Orthopedic Detail Matters

When a shoulder, knee or lumbar segment is the main driver, I spend extra time on range, strength clues and which motions reproduce symptoms. Traditional pattern diagnosis still matters, and so does the local tissue story.

Heat, Electricity and Manual Add-Ons

Not every visit needs extras. When heat, gentle electrical stimulation, cupping or gua sha would help the plan, I explain why before adding them.
