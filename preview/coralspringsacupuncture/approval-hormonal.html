Common Questions & Answers

 

Does it hurt?

The needles are super thin and most people barely feel it. Some treatments may only involve herbs & supplements.

Do I have to keep coming forever?

The vast majority of patients don’t need long-term maintenance. Once their condition is resolved, it typically stays that way.

How often do I have to come in for treatments?

Most of our patients get excellent results with treatments once a week. We will discuss this in detail at your free consultation.

Do you take my insurance?

We are in-network and take insurance from most of the major carriers. However, it depends on your particular plan, deductible and copays. To verify, please call 954-775-3765 or contact us.

Is it REALLY capable of helping my hormonal imbalance?

Definitely. Acupuncture and functional medicine helps your body use it’s own immune system and defense mechanisms to restore order. And that’s much more powerful than any man-made medicine.

How long until I see results?

Many patients begin to improve after the first session. Acupuncture and functional medicine is extremely effective at relieving hormone symptoms and even a few visits can be transformative for someone who has been suffering for years.

What are my chances of success?

Many patients report improvement in frequency and severity of their hormone symptoms. However, every individual is unique and results may vary.  Once we meet with you and complete an assessment, we will be able to best estimate the time and prognosis of outcome.

How long is a session?

Your first visit will be a little over an hour depending on the complexity of your case. Follow ups should be just under an hour.
