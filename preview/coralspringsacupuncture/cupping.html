Lift, Not Press

Most hands on work pushes into tissue. Cupping flips that. A cup placed on the skin creates suction that draws skin, fascia and muscle upward. That lift opens space between tissue layers and invites circulation into areas that feel locked. The sensation is a firm pull, and most people find it surprisingly relaxing.

Chinese medicine describes the goal as moving stagnation. A physiologist might talk about blood flow and connective tissue. Either way, the purpose is to help tight, sluggish tissue loosen up.

Ways Dr. Fields Uses Cups

Parked cups. Several cups rest in place for a few minutes over the tightest spots.

Gliding cups. With massage oil, a cup moves along a muscle like a reverse deep tissue massage.

Flash and fire cups. A flame briefly heats the air inside a glass cup before it is placed. The flame never touches the skin.

Silicone cups. Soft, squeezable cups that are easy to reposition.

Wet cupping. A traditional method using tiny skin pricks, discussed with you first and used only when appropriate.

Reading the Marks

The circles left behind can be pale pink or deep plum. They are not impact bruises. They show blood drawn toward the surface from tissue where circulation was sluggish, and Chinese medicine reads darker color as more stagnation. Most fade within several days to a week and tend to be lighter with each session.

Connective Tissue and Cupping

Fascia wraps every muscle like a thin sleeve. When layers stick together, a muscle can feel tight no matter how much you stretch. Because suction pulls rather than pushes, it may help those layers glide again. That is why gliding cups feel so different from a standard massage.

After Your Session

  • Stay warm and avoid sitting directly under air conditioning vents
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Wait a few hours before a hot shower, sauna or hot tub
  • Light activity is great, but save hard training for tomorrow

Who Should Skip Cupping

Cups are not placed on broken skin, rashes, varicose veins or acutely inflamed areas. Tell Dr. Fields if you are pregnant, bruise easily or take blood thinners. Pressure is lighter for first timers and sensitive skin.

Planning Around Events

Weddings, beach days and photo shoots matter. If visible marks would be a problem, tell Dr. Fields and he will choose areas or techniques accordingly.

Cupping for Coral Springs Athletes

Swim teams, soccer leagues, runners on the Sawgrass Nature Center trails and gym regulars all put tissue under repeated load. Many athletes use cupping between training blocks to help them feel looser. Share your training and competition calendar so Dr. Fields can time sessions for recovery rather than right before a big effort.

Hydration and Heat

South Florida heat matters after a session. Drink water, stay out of intense sun for the rest of the day and skip the hot tub that evening. Keep the treated area covered if the air conditioning is blasting, since many people feel a little more sensitive to cold drafts right after cupping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does cupping hurt?

Most people describe a strong but pleasant pull.

How long does it take?

Usually five to fifteen minutes within a broader visit.

How often can I have it?

Typically once the previous marks have faded.
