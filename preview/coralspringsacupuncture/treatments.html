An integrative treatment plan in Coral Springs

Most patients use two or three of these together. Traditional Chinese medicine treatments in Coral Springs are always reviewed against your medications.

Acupuncture and herbs

Acupuncture works quickly on pain and stress, while herbs are taken at home between visits. We check herb and drug interactions before anything is recommended.

Hands-on movement
